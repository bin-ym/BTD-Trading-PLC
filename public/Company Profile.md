### **Leadership Roles & Responsibilities:**

* **Dedicated Shift Leaders (On-Site):**
  * Stationed directly at the client’s premises to lead and supervise on-site cleaning crews.
  * Oversee punctual attendance, shift handovers, and uniform/PPE compliance.
  * Conduct continuous room-by-room inspections using structured quality checklists.
  * Serve as the immediate point of contact for the client’s facility manager to address special requests instantly.

* **Operations Manager:**
  * Directs resource allocation, equipment supply, and multi-facility logistics across Addis Ababa.
  * Coordinates staff deployment for specialized jobs, gala support, and emergency services.

* **Quality & Safety Supervisor:**
  * Enforces standard operating procedures (SOPs) and workplace safety protocols.
  * Audits chemical handling (safe dilution and storage of Vim, Dettol, and neutralizers).
  * Performs weekly client satisfaction reviews and continuous staff skill training.
